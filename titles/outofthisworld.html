Out of this World

Out of This World is a colourful side-scrolling shoot-'em-up designed by Bettertech and published by Ariolasoft under the Reaktör label in 1987 for the Commodore 64, Amstrad CPC and ZX Spectrum.

Test pilot Captain Chuck Harrison fires the RP2-16's Quark Cannon while travelling at 0.9 light speed and is hurled into the hostile Other World. Eight looping worlds combine enemy waves, collectable weapon pods and enormous end-of-world Guardians.

This browser restoration recreates the original single-player game using the preserved Spectrum loading screen, reconstructed CPC sprite artwork and Jason C. Brooke's original Commodore 64 SID music, rendered through an emulated MOS 6581.

The original source code has not yet been recovered, so the simulation is newly written while the surviving artwork, rules, credits and music remain preserved alongside it.
