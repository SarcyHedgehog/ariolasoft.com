Karateka

1984 Brøderbund Brøderbund Commodore 64 Amstrad CPC ZX Spectrum Atari 8-bit MSX MS-DOS Atari ST Apple II Commodore 16

Karateka is a martial arts action-adventure game developed and published by Broderbund in 1984. Players assume the role of a skilled martial artist on a quest to rescue a princess from an evil warlord's fortress. With its fluid animation and intuitive controls, Karateka offers a unique blend of combat and platforming gameplay.

The game features a series of challenging levels, where players must defeat enemies using various martial arts techniques while avoiding traps and obstacles. With its cinematic presentation and compelling storyline, Karateka captivated players with its immersive gameplay and memorable characters, earning it a place as a classic in the history of video games.
