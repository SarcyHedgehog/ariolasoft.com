Print Master

1985 Unison World Ariolasoft Commodore 64 PC Booter Atari ST

Print Master is a creative graphics program from Unison World that lets users design and print personalised greetings cards, signs, stationery, calendars, banners and memos without programming or drawing skills. Designs are assembled from menus of borders, graphics, layouts and fonts before adding a message and sending the result to a printer.

The Ariolasoft catalogue advertised Commodore 64 and IBM PC editions, with more than 100 prepared graphics, eleven patterns, eight fonts and eleven border designs. The surviving Ariolasoft packshot is for the Atari 520 ST edition.

Sources reviewed: Ariolasoft Autumn/Winter 1985 catalogue; surviving Ariolasoft Atari ST packaging; Unison World Print Master records.
