Hard Hat Mack

1983 Electronic Arts Electronic Arts Commodore 64 Amstrad CPC Atari 8-bit PC Booter Amstrad PCW

Hard Hat Mack is a platformer video game developed and published by Electronic Arts in 1983. Players control a construction worker named Mack who must navigate through hazardous construction sites to complete tasks. Known for its challenging gameplay and colorful graphics, Hard Hat Mack garnered positive reviews from players and critics alike.
