One-on-One

1983 Electronic Arts Electronic Arts Commodore 64 ZX Spectrum Atari 8-bit Amiga Amstrad PCW Commodore 16

One-On-One is a basketball simulation video game developed and published by Electronic Arts in 1983. Featuring a one-on-one basketball match between Larry Bird and Julius Erving, players can experience intense basketball action on their computer screens. With its realistic gameplay and smooth controls, One-On-One became a favorite among sports gaming enthusiasts.
