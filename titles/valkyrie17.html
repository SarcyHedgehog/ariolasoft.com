Valkyrie 17

1984 Ram Jam Ariolasoft Commodore 64 ZX Spectrum

Valkyrie 17 is a sci-fi action-adventure game developed by Ram Jam and published by Ariolasoft in 1984. Set in a dystopian future, players assume the role of a rebel fighter on a mission to overthrow a tyrannical regime. With its immersive storyline and captivating gameplay, Valkyrie 17 offers an unforgettable gaming experience.

The game features a mix of platforming, puzzle-solving, and combat elements, as players navigate through futuristic environments, uncovering secrets and battling enemies along the way. With its cutting-edge graphics and innovative gameplay mechanics, Valkyrie 17 pushed the boundaries of what was possible in gaming at the time, earning critical acclaim and a dedicated fanbase.
