Stealth

1984 Tracy LaGrone and Richard E. Sansom Brøderbund Commodore 64 Atari 8-bit

Stealth is a pseudo-3D rail shooter designed by Tracy LaGrone and Richard E. Sansom and published by Brøderbund in 1984. At the controls of a low-flying Stealth Starfighter, the player must penetrate hostile territory and destroy the Dark Tower, stronghold of the Council of Nine.

Warp-fighters attack from the air while radar towers, photon tanks, missiles and laser artillery defend the landscape below. Positive energy fields replenish the ship, while negative fields drain it. Ariolasoft released Stealth in Europe for Commodore 64 and Atari 8-bit computers.

Sources reviewed: Ariolasoft Autumn/Winter 1985 catalogue; original Ariolasoft Commodore 64 packaging; Brøderbund release records.
