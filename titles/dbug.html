D-Bug

1983 ChildWare Corporation Electronic Arts Commodore 64 Atari 8-bit

D-Bug is an educational action-strategy game developed by ChildWare Corporation and published by Electronic Arts in 1983 for the Commodore 64 and Atari 8-bit computers. Ariolasoft subsequently released both versions in Europe.

The game begins with a two-player contest called Gotcha! before the computer running it breaks down. Charlie Fix-It, who lives inside the machine, must locate the fault and repair the bug. The result is a game within a game that introduces the components and failure modes of a home computer through a series of diagnostic challenges.

Sources reviewed: Ariolasoft Autumn/Winter 1985 catalogue; MobyGames D-Bug release record; contemporary Electronic Arts material.
