Ariolasoft Games Archive

Marble Madness

1984 Atari Electronic Arts Commodore 64 PC Booter Amiga Apple II

Marble Madness is the home-computer adaptation of Atari Games' influential 1984 arcade game. Players race a marble through six isometric obstacle courses against a strict time limit, contending with ramps, narrow ledges, moving hazards and hostile marbles.

Electronic Arts published the home versions, with Ariolasoft handling European editions. The surviving Ariolasoft material includes Commodore 64 packaging and an Amiga disk, alongside versions for Apple II and IBM PC compatibles.
