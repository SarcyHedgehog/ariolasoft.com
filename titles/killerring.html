Killer Ring

1987 Byte Engineers Reaktor Commodore 64 Amstrad CPC

Research in progress.

Killer Ring appeared in 1987 under the Reaktor label. The catalogue credits Byte Engineers.

Known catalogue formats: Commodore 64, Amstrad CPC.

Byte Engineers was the credited name used by Tony Crowther for this work while he was contracted to Alligata Software.
