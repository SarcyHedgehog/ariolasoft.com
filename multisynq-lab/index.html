PRIVATE RESTORATION WORKSHOP

THE MULTISYNQ LAB

A small, private home for collaborative experiments that deserved to keep running.

These projects began with Croquet or Multisynq: elegant shared simulations in which the synchronized model was the source of truth. With the original network gone, I am preserving and rebuilding selected examples on Photon Realtime.

The aim is not to erase their history or pretend Photon is the same system. Each restoration keeps the original repository story, documents the compromises, and retains a deterministic model wherever that still makes sense. Expect prototypes, diagnostic controls, occasional rough edges and unapologetic geekery.
